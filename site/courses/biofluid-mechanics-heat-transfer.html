BME

Biofluid Mechanics and Heat Transfer

Fluid properties and statics, conservation laws, viscous flow in tubes, blood rheology and arterial flow, microcirculation and mass transport, then conduction, convection, radiation and bioheat transfer.

Audience
Biomedical Engineering students (no prior fluids or heat transfer background)
Textbooks
Rubenstein, Yin & Frame, Biofluid Mechanics; Datta, Biological and Bioenvironmental Heat and Mass Transfer; Young et al., A Brief Introduction to Fluid Mechanics
